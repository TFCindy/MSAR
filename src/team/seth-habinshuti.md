It started with a few of us in statistics who just enjoyed meeting up to have fun with maths. We played games, tried puzzles, and solved questions together, with no pressure and nothing official about it. Then students from other years began joining, and it became clear that a lot of people wanted a place where maths felt enjoyable.

I wanted MSAR to be a place where mathematics and statistics students support each other, build skills they can use after graduation, and see how these subjects are applied in real jobs. My hope is that it grows into the community Rwandan mathematicians and statisticians are proud to have come from.
