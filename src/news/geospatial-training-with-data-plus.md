Through MSAR's collaboration with **DATA+ Rwanda**, five selected MSAR students took part in professional training in **geospatial data analytics**.

The training covered **QGIS**, **Google Earth Engine**, **Python** and **practical spatial analysis** — specialised skills that are increasingly in demand across research, government and industry.

Specialist trainings like this extend what members learn in regular MSAR sessions. [Read more about the training](/events/geospatial-training-data-plus/).
