MSAR and **Jobra** will host a career development session on **Tuesday, 17 November 2026, from 5:00 PM to 7:00 PM** at **UR–CST, Muhabura Building, 5th Floor**.

The session is designed for MSAR members and participating students who want to take practical first steps towards the world of work:

1. **Onboarding the talent profile** — set up your Jobra profile with support.
2. **Career insights by Jobra experts** — what employers look for, and how to stand out.
3. **Perspectives from a senior data professional** — a real career journey in data.

The registration link will be shared on MSAR's official channels. [See the event details](/events/msar-jobra-career-session-2026/).
