Participants of the **MSAR Data Skills Training Program** came together to celebrate completing the program, with each graduate receiving a certificate.

The program took students through **KoboToolbox**, **Excel for data management and analysis**, **Power BI**, **AI tools** and **introductory programming** — with recent sessions focused on questionnaire programming, data collection, data cleaning, lookup functions and interpretation.

The ceremony also featured speakers who shared their own career journeys. [See photos from the ceremony](/events/data-skills-certificate-ceremony/).
