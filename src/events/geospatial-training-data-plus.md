Through MSAR's collaboration with **DATA+ Rwanda**, **five selected MSAR students** took part in professional training in geospatial data analytics.

The training covered:

- **QGIS** for creating, editing and analysing spatial data
- **Google Earth Engine** for working with satellite imagery at scale
- **Python** for automating geospatial workflows
- **Practical spatial analysis** applied to real questions

Opportunities like this extend what members learn in MSAR sessions into specialised, in-demand skills.
