The **MSAR Data Skills Training Program** is our practical training initiative at the University of Rwanda — College of Science and Technology. It takes students beyond formulas and theory into the tools that analysts use every day.

**What the program covers**

- **KoboToolbox** — designing and programming questionnaires, and collecting data in the field
- **Excel for data management and analysis** — data cleaning, lookup functions, summarising and interpreting results
- **Power BI** — turning cleaned data into clear reports and dashboards
- **AI tools and introductory programming** — using modern tools responsibly to work faster and smarter

Recent sessions focused on practical questionnaire programming, data collection, cleaning data in Excel, lookup functions and interpreting the results — always with laptops open and participants doing the work themselves.
