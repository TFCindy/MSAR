After weeks of hands-on sessions, participants of the **MSAR Data Skills Training Program** came together to celebrate what they had built. Each participant who completed the program was recognised with a certificate.

The ceremony also featured conversations with speakers who shared their own journeys — how they learned, the roles analytical skills opened up for them, and advice for students preparing for the world of work.

More than a training: a community growing together.
