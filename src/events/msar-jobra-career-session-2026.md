Practical career growth starts with the experience you build today. This joint session between MSAR and **Jobra** is designed to help mathematics and statistics students take concrete first steps towards the world of work.

Participants will be onboarded onto their **Jobra talent profile**, hear **career insights from Jobra experts**, and learn from the journey of a **senior data professional** about how analytical skills translate into real roles.

**Learn. Connect. Grow.** Bring your questions about early careers, internships, and how to present your skills to employers.
