MSAR's fellowship sessions at the **University of Rwanda Gikondo Campus** bring mathematics and statistics students together outside the lecture timetable.

Each session mixes **interactive learning**, open **discussion** and **career-oriented conversations** — a space to ask questions, learn from peers in other years, and think about how mathematics and statistics connect to life after university.
