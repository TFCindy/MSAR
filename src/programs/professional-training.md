Some skills are best learned from organisations that use them every day. Through partnerships, MSAR opens specialist training to members who are ready to go deeper.

Our collaboration with **DATA+ Rwanda** enabled **five selected MSAR students** to complete professional geospatial data analytics training covering QGIS, Google Earth Engine, Python and practical spatial analysis.
