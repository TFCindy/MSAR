The **MSAR Data Skills Training Program** is our practical training initiative at the University of Rwanda — College of Science and Technology. Sessions are built around doing: every participant works on their own laptop, with facilitators and peers on hand to help.

The program follows the full life of a dataset — from designing a questionnaire and collecting responses, through cleaning and analysis, to communicating results that someone can act on.
