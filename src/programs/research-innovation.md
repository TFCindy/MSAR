Mathematics and statistics are at their most powerful when they answer real questions. Research and innovation is a **growing area** for MSAR: our training already takes members through study design, data collection, analysis and interpretation.

We want to build on that foundation with student-led projects and collaborations. If you are a member with a research idea — or an organisation with a question that data could answer — we would like to hear from you.
