Students often know the theory but not the path. Career development at MSAR closes that gap with **guest talks**, **alumni sessions** and **career sessions with partners**.

Our collaboration with **Jobra** focuses on connecting students with professional opportunities, career insights, talent profiling, mentorship and industry exposure. We are also exploring further career initiatives, including possible internship opportunities in 2027 — nothing is confirmed yet, and members will hear first when there is news.
