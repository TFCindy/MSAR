MSAR works with institutions and organisations — including the **University of Rwanda**, **DATA+ Rwanda** and **Jobra** — to bring real-world perspective into students' learning.

Alongside partnerships, gatherings such as the **MSAR Yearly Dinner** bring members, alumni and guests together to celebrate progress and set direction for the year ahead.
