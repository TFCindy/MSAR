MSAR began as a handful of statistics students who enjoyed meeting to solve problems together — no pressure, nothing official. That spirit still sits at the centre of the association.

Today, regular sessions at UR–CST and our **fellowship sessions at the Gikondo campus** bring students together for interactive learning, discussion, quizzes and problem-solving across different years of study.
